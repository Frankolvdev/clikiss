# Fondos de navegación

Generados con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`. Se inspeccionaron los resultados y se optimizaron a WebP de 960 píxeles de ancho con `scripts/optimize-nav-images.cjs`. Los originales se conservan en la ubicación de generación; las imágenes utilizadas por la aplicación están dentro del proyecto.

## Archivos finales

- `public/images/experience-connections.webp`
- `public/images/experience-adventure.webp`
- `public/images/experience-moments.webp`

Para Aventura se utilizó finalmente una fotografía SFW de lencería como producto, sin personas ni desnudez. Sustituye al fondo anterior de vestido rojo.

## Prompts finales

### Conexiones

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide photographic background for a premium dating app navigation tile. A romantic adult couple, both around 30 years old, sharing a warm affectionate moment on a rooftop at dusk, elegant everyday clothing, natural candid expressions. Cinematic red and warm golden city bokeh, deep black shadows. Landscape 3:2 composition with the couple on the right half, atmospheric dark negative space on the left and bottom for white UI text added later. No text, logos, frame or interface. Natural editorial photography, not a glossy stock advertisement.

### Aventura

Use case: product-mockup. A tasteful SFW fashion product photograph, flat lay of an opaque black satin lingerie camisole and matching high-waisted shorts, neatly arranged on dark velvet beside a folded burgundy satin robe. No person, no body parts, no mannequin, no sexual activity. Refined boutique apparel photography with warm metallic gold accents and rich red accent light against near-black background. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, garments grouped on right half, darker negative space on left for a website category label added later. No text, logos or typography.

### Momentos

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide photographic background for a social feed navigation tile in a premium dating app. A small group of clearly adult friends around age 30 enjoying a rooftop evening, one adult woman taking a candid photo of her friends with her smartphone. Warm and spontaneous, lively but tasteful. Cinematic warm gold lights, subtle rich red accents, deep black night background. Landscape 3:2 composition, people on the right side, dark negative space on left and bottom for white UI text added later. No text, logos, app interface or collage.
